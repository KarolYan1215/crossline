# Voice input (2026-11-02)

> Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-04. Add new notes for this area here, not to `CLAUDE.md`.

Design, research and the person's decisions: `docs/voice-input.md`. Every free-text box carries a microphone: wrap the `<input>`/`<textarea>` in `VoiceField` (`apps/web/app/ui/voice/voice-input.tsx`, kind `conversation | interview | note | form`), or put a `VoiceButton` with a `target` beside it where the box has its own row of controls (the composer). Not on search, URLs, hosts, codes, numbers, passwords, keys, sign-in forms or remote-login keys. A new free-text box gets one in the same change.
- **Engine** (`chooseEngine` in `voice-model.ts`): the browser's own Web Speech API first; Crossline's SenseVoice where it is missing (Firefox, an iPhone home-screen app — WebKit bug 225298) or answered `network`/`service-not-allowed`/`language-not-supported` in this tab. Hosted: the web writes 16 kHz PCM to `<data>/dictation/upload/<ws>/`, the worker's `/v1/voice/transcribe` moves it to `dictation/inbox/` and waits up to 90 s for `outbox/<id>.json`, which the `crossline-transcriber` container writes ahead of recordings (priority lock in `transcriber.ts`, heartbeat `dictation/transcriber.alive` every 30 s, the model kept loaded 10 minutes after a dictation). Local install: the worker runs SenseVoice itself after the person's consented download (`/v1/voice/model`). The clip is deleted on read, leftovers swept after 2 minutes, `dictation/` is outside backups.
- **Text**: the raw text goes in at the caret at once (`insertDictation`); `crossline-dictation-corrector` (low tier, interactive priority) then fixes misheard words, names, numbers, punctuation and fillers only — `validateDictationCorrection` refuses a rewrite — and replaces the span only while the person has not edited it (`spanNow`), with 「已纠正 N 处 · 恢复原文」. Context: the person's line, project, task and milestone names and their **dictation wordings**: edits made to a dictated span become `dictation`-source preferences (`lexiconEdits`, scope `dictation`, read only by the corrector, deletable in Settings → 个性化).
- **Settings** → 通用 「语音输入」 (`voice.preferences`): microphone on/off, language (跟随界面 / 普通话 / English / 粤语), correction on/off (default on; uses the person's quota). A first-use notice per engine says where the audio goes.
- **A muted system microphone is said at once** (`microphoneMuted`): beside the recording a second, unprocessed capture is read for 1.2 s (not on Safari); a muted track or exact zeros put 「这台电脑的麦克风处于静音…」 on the recording bar and in place of 「没有听清」. The owner's microphone array was muted in Windows and every recogniser answered `no-speech`; a fallback that re-sent such recordings to SenseVoice was built on the wrong cause, added steps, and was reverted.
- Tests: `packages/core/src/dictation.test.ts`, `apps/worker/src/dictation.test.ts` (local and hosted paths, path traversal, wrong workspace, correction kept raw on a rewrite, lexicon), `apps/web/app/ui/voice/voice-model.test.ts`.
