# The profile-first loop: interview, dates, milestones, research, recordings

> Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-04. Add new notes for this area here, not to `CLAUDE.md`.

A new account no longer lands on four placeholder goals. It lands on `/onboarding`, and everything downstream is built from what that learns. `ensureSeeded` seeds the template library and an empty plan **only**; the example milestones, relations and work orders exist behind `CROSSLINE_SEED_EXAMPLES=1`, which `vitest.config.ts`, `playwright.config.ts` and `scripts/run-e2e.ts` set because the suites and the visual baselines were recorded against that board. **Every page but the interview, settings, sign-in and `/offline` is gated on the start standard** (decided 2026-10-12, `docs/loop-and-scout.md` §7): `gateUntilReady(ctx)` (`apps/web/lib/start-gate.ts`), called right after `requireSessionContext()`, redirects to `/onboarding` until `readyToStart` holds, and no account is exempt — one with work on its board is held too, so the first schedule anyone sees rests on what they said. It replaced `needsOnboarding`, which let any account with a session or a fact through. API routes are not gated (the worker, local tools and offline replay need them). The example board meets the standard (`seedExampleReadiness`, `apps/web/lib/example-readiness.ts`: lines with descriptions and adopted goals, the default hours as the interview's proposal, one no-deadline project per line), or the suites would land on the interview; `seedReadyToStart` in `apps/web/lib/test-support.ts` is the same for tests. `apps/web/lib/start-gate.test.ts`, `apps/web/app/empty-account.test.tsx` (an empty account is sent to the interview; a ready one with no milestone or work still renders every page) and the e2e test 「未达可开始标准时打开今日页回到访谈…」 (a second account of its own, `e2e/accounts.ts` `signUpAccount`) are the guards. `scripts/start-readiness.ts <copy.db>` prints what each workspace of a ledger copy still misses (it refuses the live ledger).

**The interview is a ledger conversation, not a form** (`apps/worker/src/onboarding-session.ts`, `packages/core/src/onboarding-turn-schema.ts`). Each turn asks several questions at once with 8–14 candidate options, and every `confirmed` fact must cite the numbered sentences it rests on (see 「Numbered evidence」 below); anything the model worked out is `inferred` with no citation and the store never lets a guess overwrite a confirmed fact.

**Two layers: the person waits only for the dialogue (2026-10-19, P2 of the redesign; docs/onboarding-v3.md 「访谈的两层」).** The dialogue (`crossline-onboarding`, its own thread, top tier) writes only `reply`, `questions`, `coverage`, `nextAction`, `stage` (`ONBOARDING_DIALOGUE_SCHEMA`; ids are per-turn enums, `onboardingDialogueSchema(ids)`; a question naming a project not yet on record is kept as about its line) and reads the person's words and files as they are. The extraction (`crossline-onboarding-extract`, mid tier, `planning` priority; `apps/worker/src/onboarding-extract.ts`) runs beside it on a fresh thread each time: the replies and files not yet extracted as numbered sentences, a short index of the facts on record, the lines' profiles and the portrait, plus two lookups (`search_evidence`, `read_sentences`) whose results are citable; it writes facts, projects, dates, attributes, goal edits, hours, feeds and the portrait (`ONBOARDING_EXTRACT_SCHEMA`) through `persistExtraction` (`onboarding-persist.ts`, the only writer of those now). Every turn starts it at once (`requestExtraction`: one per workspace, a request while it runs makes it go round again, files over one run's budget follow at once); a round says `ready_for_milestones` only after waiting for it, so the checklist is read with this reply's facts on record. Later dialogue turns carry `recorded` (facts written since the last turn). State: `onboarding.extract:<session>` (`upTo`, failures, `retryAt`; backoff 1/5/30 minutes, retried by the tick's `onboardingExtractPass`) and `onboarding.extract.docs` (file → first sentence id extracted at, 0 queued, -1 no text); `extractBacklog` (`packages/db/src/onboarding-extract.ts`) is what `/api/onboarding/state` returns as `organizing`, and 「开始使用」 waits for it unless it keeps failing. The runner's `extract` option: `background` (the app), `await` (scripts and tests), `off` (the sponsor's sample interview, which measures the dialogue). `ONBOARDING_TURN_SCHEMA` is gone; both layers are checked by `validateOnboardingTurn` with the other layer's fields emptied (`validateOnboardingDialogue`, `validateOnboardingExtraction`). The test runtime answers each layer from its own queue (`fakeRuntime(…, { routes })`, matched on the extraction's context version). After review: conversations from before it start at their last reply (`backfillExtractWatermarks` at worker start, once per workspace, skipped where the extraction already ran); a run reads at most 6 replies; the tick's retry is core spend and obeys the planning pause; date ids are made from content (`withContentIds`), since each fresh thread numbers from d1; an old conformance record's `ONBOARDING_TURN_SCHEMA` run counts for both layers (`LEGACY_CASES`); a request during a run passes its conversation to the next round (`preferred`, bypassing backoff), and the dialogue waits for *its reply* (`extractionReached`; up to 30 s when a blocking app question is open); files shown in a line interview are queued and extracted with the main one; a re-read queues files at the dialogue's pace; a file queued again during a run is not marked done by it; a throw goes through the backoff and one answer's writes are one transaction (`CrosslineStore.atomically`).

**Five parts, a progress record, observations and payoffs (2026-10-19, after testers found it too detailed; docs/onboarding-v3.md 「精简与回报」).** The main interview is orientation → lines → situation → compass → energy; the details stage is gone (line interviews keep `details`), and a project's checklist item is `deferred` (`interviewComplete`/`interviewOwnedOpen` ignore it): what is missing is asked when its task is planned or on 待决事项. Per line at most two or three core deadline questions; a rough time is the person's word; a deadline worked out from files is an inferred `deadline` 「推断：…」 that the deadline gap offers as 「就按 …」 (`DEADLINE_GUESS_PREFIX`); a deadline gap blocks only while no project of its line knows its deadline. The dialogue writes `progress {part, covered, open, partDone, next}` every round, kept in the session and handed back next round (`progressLines`) and shown in the right column (`progressView`); it is steered by the prompt, never derived by code. Each round's context is the short ledger (`apps/worker/src/onboarding-pace.ts` `paceContext`: progress, ≤8 open checklist items, a ≤15-line `abstract` built by `interviewAbstract`, the last 15 questions, the latest ratings) instead of the whole checklist, ledger and `recorded`. In the deep parts the dialogue writes one `insight` connecting two of the person's replies and naming the pattern between them in one short clause (no advice, no verdict on character); `checkInsight` keeps it only when every quote is in the reply it names (else the card is dropped, the round kept); 准/不准 (`POST /api/onboarding/insight`, `onboarding.insights`) goes to the next round and the extraction, and 「准」 in all three deep parts confirms the portrait (`portraitConfirmedByInsights`). Payoffs are computed (`packages/core/src/onboarding-pace.ts`, `weekCapacity` in `apps/web/lib/onboarding-board.ts`): the file-read list (dates adopted at once, `adoptPendingDates`; removable item by item, `changeAdoptedDate`), the capacity note (`capacityConflict`) and, once the start standard stands, the week's draft (`skeletonDraft`, weights from shares or rank). Ending: the dialogue's 「ready」 marks the session finishing (`markFinishing`, `EXTRACT_FINISHING_SETTING`), which the extractor reads as a completed interview (finalize every deep section, write the synthesis; a run is made for it even with nothing new, at most `FINISH_RUNS_MAX`); the synthesis is a deferred item (`portrait_synthesis`), never asked; the ending round waits for the extraction at most `FINISH_WAIT_MS` (90 s), after which the portrait's items do not turn it back. Only replies to the opening and the front stages, and files, hold 「开始使用」 (`extractBacklog().frontReplies`): on the sponsor model a run took 5–7 minutes and the deep stages' replies queued behind.

**What the interview hears becomes settings, hours and the energy curve (2026-10-30, the owner: 「系统功能设置应该在访谈中就根据用户偏好设置好」; docs/onboarding-v3.md 「访谈到设置」).** An audit found only the proposed hours reached any feature; quiet hours, the brief's time, block length, a daily cap, a buffer and what the assistant may do were fact text nobody read. Now: the extraction returns `settings[]` (`{key, target, value, refs}`, keys limited to `INTERVIEW_SETTING_KEYS` in `packages/core/src/interview-settings.ts`: `notify.level`, `notify.quietHours`, `notify.briefTime`, the four packing numbers, `mail.replyDrafts`, `agent.category`, `agent.autoHandoff`; never a consent or one object's setting), each resting on one of the person's replies; `persistExtraction` checks each with `settingProblem` and keeps them in `onboarding.settings` (`mergeInterviewSettings`: a later statement of the same key comes back pending). The start confirmation lists them ticked, with the person's words, and applies the ticked ones through `applySettingChange` (`settleInterviewSettings` in `apps/web/lib/start-confirm.ts`); after the start they are one `interview_settings` ask (全部应用 / 保持现状). Two settings are new and in the catalog: `notify.quietHours` (`HH:MM-HH:MM`, may wrap midnight, default 23:00–08:00 — read by the web's nudges as `night` and by the worker's urgent notifications) and `notify.briefTime` (`HH:MM`, else the first available hour), both on Settings → 时间与提醒 (`NotifyTimes`). The dialogue asks once, in the energy part, how the app should work with the person, and asks their usual sleep and wake; the extraction records `person/sleep_window` as `HH:MM-HH:MM`, which `statedSleep` makes the energy curve's night until real nights are recorded, re-sends the hours with `energy` split when the person names their clearest or most tired hours (both evaluations had every window `medium`) starting from the hours on record, which it is now shown (`hours` in its context; a run that read only the sleep-and-running reply had rebuilt the week from it and lost the weekend), records a weekly habit at a set time as a fixed weekly item, and records the number aimed for on any project measured by one as `target_grade`. The question ledger lets a question answered 「不确定」 be asked once more (`unsureAnswer`; the owner-profile evaluation asked one generic question thirteen rounds running because every re-ask of an unsure start item was dropped as answered), and a round with nothing left asks the first open start item (`withSomethingToAnswer`). The milestone architect is not asked for a fixed-schedule line whose projects are all daily work with no deadline (`onlyDailyWork`), and a chain whose last score line sits under an earlier one is `MILESTONE_THRESHOLD_REGRESSES` (soft). `scripts/interview-handoff.ts <eval dir> [--apply-settings] [--milestones]` reads what an evaluation left for the rest of the app; `scripts/onboarding-eval.ts --opening <card.json>` runs any opening card (`scripts/personas/freelancer-creator.*`).

**The profile module: narrative first, cards, claims with privacy (2026-10-31, P1 of docs/profile-and-agent-kit.md).** A new main interview runs flow v2 (`coverage.flow`: narrative → compass → energy → situation → orientation → lines → details); a session that already had a round keeps its flow. Code puts at most two cards of at most three items in a round (`cardsFor`, `CARDS_PER_ROUND`, `ITEMS_PER_CARD`; Chinese interviews only) into the assistant message's `content.cards`: Mini-IPIP 20, 18REST 12 (CC BY 4.0, in place of the RIASEC Markers), SWLS 5 on five points, six value tradeoffs — our own Chinese wording, not yet back-translated (`packages/core/src/profile-instruments.ts`). A tap comes back as an answer line read without a model (`scaleAnswerLine`/`readCardAnswers`); words in a card's box are mapped to 1–5 by the extraction (`by: "mapped"`). Claims (`profile-claims.ts`): the cards' are computed (`measured` only when every item was tapped; a domain with an unanswered item says nothing), the portrait writer's (`PortraitReport.claims`, portrait schema 6) are kept only when their quote is in the person's replies word for word (`resolveWrittenClaims` → `profile.claims`). Each report section is `public | agent | private` (`REPORT_PRIVACY_SETTING`, 张力 private by default); a claim the person marks 不符合 (`profile.claimStatus`) stays on their page, never reaches `exportableClaims` and is handed to the next writer run as `rejectedClaims`. The portrait page's 「逐条核对」 (`app/portrait/profile-sections.tsx`, `/api/profile/report`) sets privacy, judges claims and re-answers items. `scripts/persona-eval.ts` walks the interview as made-up people (a hidden core, a first-person life sheet under `scripts/personas/`, `crossline-eval-persona` v2 answering cards and observations) on the developer's model; server action `persona`/`persona-report`.

**Time tiers (2026-10-31, the owner's decision; docs/onboarding-v3.md 「时间挡位」, `packages/core/src/onboarding-depth.ts`).** The person picks how thorough the interview is on the opening card (「访谈问多细？」) and can change it above the conversation (`onboarding.depth`, `POST /api/onboarding/depth`, read every turn; unset reads as `quick`). `quick` (about 50 minutes) is the interview cut down for time on 2026-10-19 and 2026-10-31; `standard` (about 80) and `thorough` (about 120) bring back what those cuts took, through `DEPTH_PACE`: the model's questions a round (3/4/5), room for the app's non-blocking ones (4/6/8), rating-card items a round (3/6/6), rounds before a part is told to move on (`stagePace` `after`, 2/3/4), each project's goal, deadline and evidence asked in the interview from `standard` (`project:` not `deferred`) and a course's grading and class times from `thorough` (`project_course:`, split out of `project:`), the portrait's grounding holding the end again from `standard` (`portraitHolds`), and in `thorough` the details part at the end of `stageOrder` (`stageOrderFor`, `interviewParts(flow, depth)`, shown as a sixth part). The turn carries `depth`; the Skill's 「How thorough」 section says what each tier asks more of (follow-ups on deep answers, more options, more deadlines, the five-year Tuesday and ten-years questions, the per-project details). The right column's minutes scale with the tier (`minutesLeft`). The rating cards are per tier too (`DepthPace.cards`, 2026-11-05, W-186): `quick` asks a short set of whole dimensions — conscientiousness, neuroticism and the six value tradeoffs, 14 of the 43 items (`cardBank("short")`) — and the `profile_cards` start item counts that set; `standard` and up ask all 43. The part count is one function (`progressView`: the round's `stage` in `interviewParts(flow, depth)`; the model's progress record counts only while it names that part), read by both the bar above the conversation (`StageBar` gets the state's `progress`) and the right column, and a main interview with no round yet reads as flow v2 (W-186: the opening said 1/5, the first round 1/6, and the bar and the right column disagreed). Each round's context carries `fixedSchedule`, one line per weekday of every weekly arrangement with its own time (`fixedScheduleLines`; W-183: the abstract had only their count and the dialogue misstated a class time), and the stage's open question, which the app adds after the ledger's repeat check, gives way to the next stage's when the ledger says it was answered or asked twice (`withFreshOpenQuestion`, `openStageQuestion(…, askable)`; W-184). 「三个五年版本」 and the archetype action list stay out: they were cut by the 2026-10-08 design, not for time.

**The portrait by section (2026-10-19, P3).** `PROFILE_SECTIONS`: `situation` (problem, gravity, designable, reframes), `compass` (workView, lifeView, consistency, trueNorth, values), `energy` (energyMap, flow, says, does, anchor, realWant), `synthesis` (portrait, coreConflict, allocation). The extraction writes `profileSections` — each section whole or `null`, with `refs` — only when new material bears on it; `profileTask` (`portraitTask` in onboarding-extract.ts) asks it to rewrite a deep section whole once the conversation has moved past its stage (`finalize`, read back through the lookups) and to write the synthesis only once all three are final (`synthesize`); a synthesis written early is ignored (`mergeProfileSections`). `profile.sections` keeps each section's sentences and whether it is final. The confirmation fingerprint is the synthesis only (`portraitFingerprint`: portrait + coreConflict); a three-part fingerprint recorded before is still compared the old way.

**Numbered evidence (2026-10-19; `packages/core/src/sentences.ts`, `onboarding-refs.ts`, table `evidence_sentences` in migration 16).** The interview no longer asks the model to copy words: every reply and file it is shown arrives as numbered sentences (「[41] 论文 12 月中投稿」), the answer cites numbers in `refs` (facts, attribute candidates, goal edits, feeds, dates), and `resolveOnboardingRefs` fills `sourceRef`/`sourceQuote` from the ledger, so a quote is always a span of the source and everything downstream reads the same fields as before (with the ids in `profile_facts.source_sentences`). Times are minutes after midnight (`startMinute`/`endMinute`, `clockOf`). A verbatim quote had been the commonest reason an interview turn was sent back. Segmentation is the Unicode sentence algorithm through `Intl.Segmenter` (keeps `9.00`, `3.5`, `U.S.` whole), plus three rules that make its mistakes harmless rather than trying to make it right: segments keep their offsets (nothing is lost), fragments under four letters or digits join a neighbour, a run past 120 characters is cut at a space or comma; lines are cut first, so a syllabus row is one sentence with its date. Ids are per workspace, given on first use (`ensureEvidence`), never renumbered and never given twice: they come from a high-water mark in `workspace_counters` (`#nextNumber`), because `MAX(id)+1` handed a removed file's numbers to the next reply while the thread still held the old ones. A file read again retires its old sentences and gets new ids, and the runner shows it to the thread again; removing a file blanks and retires its sentences and unhooks facts that cited them. The runner indexes only what the thread has seen: a reply, a file this turn shows, and an earlier file up to the last number a turn showed of it (`shownUpTo` in the session's coverage), a re-read included; a file shown before numbered evidence existed is shown again, numbered. A strict citation check refuses only a number the model was not shown (`ONBOARDING_REF_UNKNOWN`) or a confirmed fact citing nothing (`ONBOARDING_REF_REQUIRED`); after the repairs such an item is dropped and the rest kept. Whether a fact's numbers appear in its sentences is a warning only (`citationWarnings` → `onboarding.citation_warned`), compared after `numbersIn` reads `9.00`, `9：00`, `九点半`, `下午3点` as the same numbers, because people do not type to a format. Facts written before it got their sentences once at worker start (`backfillFactEvidence`, marker `evidence.backfill.v1`). The same migration fixed `src-N`: it was a position in the upload list, so removing one file (folder exclusions do this in bulk) renumbered every later file and stored facts and dates pointed at the wrong one; each document now keeps `ref_no` from upload, never reused after a removal (the same counter; `documentsByRef` reads it; older backups are numbered on restore). **The question ledger (2026-10-19; `packages/core/src/question-ledger.ts`, `apps/worker/src/question-ledger.ts`, table `question_ledger` in migration 17).** Every question put to the person is kept once, numbered per workspace, with its status (待问 `pending` / 未答 `open` / 已答 `answered` / 按假设 `assumed` / 不再问 `dropped`) and answer: the interview's own (`iq:<session>:<round seq>:<id>`), the app's own interview questions (`gap:<id>`, registered as `pending` before each turn and dropped when their cause goes) and 待决事项, step questions included (`ask:<id>`, synced on the web's ten-minute attention timer and answered in `answerAsk`). An answer is read from the cards' own form (`【question】选择：…`, `answersInReply`) with the sentences it was given in; an answered question is never reopened. Each interview turn is shown the ledger (`questionLedger`, `ledgerLines`: 「#12 [已答] … → …」) and every question it writes names the number it repeats (`repeats`, 0 = new): an answered one asked again is dropped from the round, and so is a question within `REPEAT_SIMILARITY` (0.85 bigram Dice) of an answered one whatever number it gave (`onboarding.questions_repeated`); an app question the model asked in its own words is not added beside it. This is the general form of what `gapCoveredBy` did by word patterns for deadlines, which stays as a fallback. `pnpm doctor:ledger` checks dangling sentence ids, sentences whose reply or file is gone and duplicate or missing document numbers (and no longer reports a phantom 「等待项指向不存在的作业」 on every ledger: that query returned a count row). The messages, profile facts, projects and uploaded `source_documents` are all in SQLite, so a reload or another device resumes where it stopped. **The runtime forgets a conversation when it restarts** — a stored `codexThreadId` then answers `thread not found`, which is a JSON-RPC error object that used to surface as an anonymous `SKILL_RUNTIME_FAILED` and wedged the interview forever. `runContinuedTurn` now names it `SKILL_THREAD_LOST`, and the runner opens a fresh thread seeded with the profile, every file and the conversation so far (`history`, user lines carrying their `reply-N` id and their numbered sentences). Any new place that stores a thread id must handle that code the same way.

**Long runs stay alive on the wire.** A caller's `fetch` gives up when no byte arrives for five minutes (undici's default), and a Skill is allowed ten; a one-turn interview with a long history takes about seven. The supervisor therefore answers the interview turn, date extraction, milestone proposal and diligence routes through `sendJsonWhenReady`: status line at once, a space every twenty seconds, JSON at the end. Every outcome of those routes is already a typed `{state:"blocked", code}` in the body, so the status is 200 either way and a throw becomes one more blocked result. Do not "simplify" it back to `sendJson(await work())`.

**Model routing** (`packages/codex/src/model-policy.ts`): one table maps each skill to a tier, and a tier to `{model, effort}` — top `gpt-5.6-terra`/high (interview, milestone architect, backplanner, flywheel), mid `gpt-5.6-terra`/medium (analysis, materials, gate, weekly insight, diligence), low `gpt-5.6-terra`/low (date extraction, background facts, explainer, assistant) — one model at three efforts since 2026-10-20 (the owner: the sol/terra/luna table spent the quota too fast in testing). `CROSSLINE_MODEL_TOP|MID|LOW="model[:effort]"` override a tier and `CROSSLINE_MODEL_ROUTING=off` disables routing. The model goes in `thread/start` and, with the effort, in **every** `turn/start`, because a continued turn inherits the thread's model unless told. **模型强度** (2026-10-31, Settings → 模型连接, shown for a ChatGPT sign-in only; `MODEL_EFFORT_SETTING` `lower | default | higher` in core `model-effort.ts`, `/api/settings/model-effort`): the worker hands it to the runner (`setEffortPreferenceProvider`) and `runRouted` moves each fitted route's effort one step (`shiftEffort`, to the nearest effort the model's catalogue lists; low/medium/high without one) on the account's own Codex only — never a provider or the developer's model — and ignores 更强 while the spend mode is not `normal`. The account menu at the top right shows the 小帮手's figure (`useHelper`, `HelperFace`), the plain icon until one exists.

**Dates from files are a batch, adopted at once since 2026-10-19** (`import_dated_items` is `automatic`; the reader's low-confidence items are left out and every item can be removed or added on the file-read list) (`packages/core/src/date-schema.ts`, `apps/worker/src/onboarding-session.ts`, `apps/web/lib/imported-dates.ts`). There is **no separate date-extractor Skill**: the interview turn already reads the files, so it returns the dates in a `fileDates` field of its own answer (`persistFileDates`), and the model that reads the syllabus is the one that knows today's date and the person's zone. A turn that re-reads the files replaces what nobody has answered yet; any other turn adds to it (`mergeDateExtractions`, same id → newer) — every turn used to replace it, and one that heard 「12–14 点午饭」 threw away an unanswered batch with both ECE 548 midterms. Weekly arrangements wholly outside hours the person stated and belonging to no project (meals, sleep) are left out (`withoutOffHours`), since the available hours already leave them out and as items they were offered for confirmation and for the calendar. Each date cites the numbered sentence it was read from, which must contain the day itself (`quoteSupportsDate` accepts `10/27`, `27th Oct.`, `Oct 27`, `10月27日`; the year is not required). `sanitizeDateExtraction` drops an item that cannot be verified and reports it in `dropped` instead of failing the whole answer — the first real run lost a hundred good rows to one misquoted line and a four-minute repair. The quote comparison ignores punctuation and spacing only (`squash` keeps letters and digits): the model copying `27th Oct.` as `27th Oct` is the right quote, and refusing it dropped a real midterm. Weekly patterns are expanded in code (`expandRecurring`), never by the model, and only today-and-later reaches the calendar. A batch is two ledger events (`dates.extracted`, `dates.decided`); `extract({replace:true})` re-reads the files of a batch nobody has answered and retires it once the new one exists. Confirmed timed items become read-only plan blocks `import:<batch>:<item>` that calendar sync never touches (it only rewrites `graph_calendar:` blocks); untimed ones become `date_*` profile facts.

**Nothing a reading drops is silent (W-228, 2026-11-05).** In a reply (never in a file) a day named relative to when it was written — 「明天」「下周五」「13号」, Chinese numerals, `next Friday` — counts as stated (`relativeDays`); an arrangement across midnight is split there (`acrossMidnight`); a start in words or more than 120 days back starts today. `persistFileDates` calls `sanitizeDateExtraction` with `allowEmpty`, so a reading that verifies nothing still records its drops; every reading's drops (with a `code`, worded per code on the file-read list) are a `dates.unrecorded` event, and what keeping an extraction's answer pruned (`prunedOnboardingItems`) is an `onboarding.unrecorded` event. The dialogue's next round gets them as `notRecorded` and asks for the missing part instead of saying 「已记下」. `withoutOffHours` keeps the arrangements of every line, not only a hard line's. `latestFileDates` reads every batch still in force (not only the newest), minus items the person removed; a dated block replaces a weekly block in its slot whichever batch came first. A file longer than one run reads continues where it stopped (`onboarding.extract.offsets`) and is done only after its last part. A reply over 12,000 characters is refused, not cut. A file's relative day (「下周五补课」) is not guessed: it becomes an undated item with a `relative:` note and `profileGaps` asks which day it is (W-230). An extraction list filled to its schema cap (`listsAtLimit`, `LIMITED_LISTS`) is recorded as `LIMIT_REACHED`, so the date list and the next round say more may have been said. The same rule elsewhere: a recording digest names the minutes cut from a long transcript, the commitments past the capture/wait caps and the items it could not verify (`## 没有整理到的`, `notRecorded` on `recording.digested`); background facts drop only the bad fact; the box says when a card is at 8 actions or screenshots past 3 were not read, and a dismissed card that did nothing still feeds its line to the fact extraction; a pending date that passed while waiting is listed as passed.

**Milestones are anchored to the person** (`packages/core/src/milestone-plan.ts`, `apps/worker/src/milestone-proposer.ts`). When the context carries a `profile`, each proposal needs a `plan` — `key`, `stage`, `deadline`, `baseline`, `target`, `stakeholders`, `sourceFactIds`, `dependsOn` — and `validateMilestonePlans` refuses what it can check: a generic or too-short title, a missing or past or >18-month deadline, an empty target, no source fact (or only inferred ones), an unknown fact id, no `outcome` criterion, fewer than three or more than five per covered line, a later stage due before an earlier one, an unchained stage, a dependency that is not earlier. The refusal's `detail` names the milestone, because a bare code gives the model nothing to repair. Anchors live in `milestone_anchors` keyed by milestone id (not in the versioned `milestones` row), so revising a milestone does not lose them. Proposing archives untouched `*-starter` milestones, the relations between them and untouched seeded work orders; an accepted order is history and stays. Accepted diligence proposals reach the architect as `diligence[]`.

**Web research is checked, then opened** (the opportunities question assumes no status or place: it is given the person's confirmed `person` facts beside the line's and lists only what they plausibly qualify for — it once asked 「是否对在读学生开放」 of everyone and offered a Hangzhou founder UC Berkeley's student fund) (`packages/core/src/diligence-schema.ts`, `apps/worker/src/diligence.ts`). The contract is built around sources: a source is a public https page (no IP literal, local name or port), every finding cites sources by id, every proposal rests on findings and on the person's own fact ids, and the investment line may not contain a trade call or price target. After the model answers, `verifySource` actually requests each page — manual redirects, every hop re-checked against a DNS lookup so a hostname cannot lead to a private address — and drops a source that does not open or whose content does not match its claimed title, together with every finding that depended only on it. `pageSupports` then requires the Latin names in a finding's claim to appear in the page it cites (a real run filed a Hult Prize deadline under NVIDIA's page); figures are not compared, because `2026-11-15` and "Nov 15" are the same date. A report with nothing left is a failure and writes no conclusions. **A report may not call a stated fact unknown** (W-250): every line's card description goes in as a `line-description-*` fact (this line's first; the research line once said 「未记录是否为在读学生」 because the schooling was written only on the student line's card), and `factsIgnored` flags a gap or proposal that says the person's status or place is 未记录/未提供/unknown while a fact on that topic has a value. A flagged gap alone is dropped without another web run (`keepEarly`); a flagged proposal is refused (`DILIGENCE_FACT_IGNORED`, the detail names the fact) for one repair turn, and if the repair still ignores it the proposal is dropped (`softAccept`). Each line is researched at most weekly, in the background, only once the interview is complete or a report already exists, and stops when the budget is in economy mode. Nothing a report proposes becomes a goal until the person accepts it. **The runtime executes web search through `codex-code-mode-host`, which it starts from beside its own executable**; a fork built without it answers every search with `failed to spawn code-mode host` and the model reports "the research tool is unavailable". `webResearchBlocker()` checks for the file up front and names the path. Building it from `vendor/codex` needs a V8 prebuilt archive from GitHub; the installed Codex app's `bin\codex-code-mode-host.exe` was copied in instead (a newer build than the pinned 0.155.0 — it worked, but re-verify after any runtime bump).

**Recordings are transcribed on this machine** (`apps/worker/src/transcribe.ts`). `TranscriptionEngine` isolates the model and the decoder, so the consent gate, the status the screen reads, the 3-hour limit and the timestamped transcript are tested without either. The model (`onnx-community/whisper-small`, 8-bit, about 255 MB) is fetched only from a request carrying `consent: true`, which the screen sends only from the button that names the source and size; recognition afterwards runs with `allowRemoteModels = false`. **The language is detected, not assumed**: transformers.js does not detect it (it logs "defaulting to English"), so `detectLanguage` sums the first decoding step's scores for each language token over three half-minute clips (10%, 40%, 70% in) and the transcript's first line names the result; `CROSSLINE_TRANSCRIBE_LANGUAGE` pins it. **Recordings from the phone, all day, without uploading** (2026-10-20; `docs/phone-recording.md`, `apps/worker/src/recordings.ts`, `vad.ts`, `recording-digest.ts`, `packages/core/src/recordings.ts`, `recording-digest.ts`, settings card `app/settings/recording-card.tsx`, `POST /api/settings/recordings`): under its own consent 录音自动转写 (`RECORDING_CONSENT`, off by default, machine owner only), the folder pass queues recordings (`RECORDING_FILE`: m4a/mp3/wav/aac/opus/ogg/webm/flac, ≤500 MB) found in an authorized folder once their mtime is two minutes old (`audio: "queued"` on the folder index entry); `startRecordings` runs `transcribeConcurrency()` lanes (2–4 by cores and free memory, `CROSSLINE_TRANSCRIBE_CONCURRENCY`; lanes claim files so none is done twice) over one cached offline Whisper pipeline — ONNX already uses every core, so a second lane overlaps work rather than doubling it. `transcribeSpeech` runs an energy VAD first (adaptive floor + 10 dB, clamped to −50…−30 dBFS; no Silero, which would be a second model download) and sends only the speech, packed into ≤10-minute pieces whose times map back into the recording, then drops subtitle stock phrases and a line looping a third time (`withoutHallucinations`); a recording with no speech is `silent` and never reaches the model. The transcript is a `text` source document `<folder>/<file> 转写.txt` (start time from mtime less length); the audio is never copied — only name, length and sha256, in a `recording.transcribed`/`recording.silent` event keyed by the audio's sha256, which is what stops a renamed or re-synced copy being transcribed again. Between pieces the worker rests twice the piece's time while the person is at the computer in the day (`recordingPace`: idle from the activity reader's latest `idleMs`, held in memory; 23:00–07:00 or ≥5 min idle is full speed). Each transcript is then digested by Skill `crossline-recording-digest` (low tier, intake): kinds meeting/lecture/call/conversation/self_note are kept, small_talk/media/noise are not (a near-empty transcript is noise by rule, no model); a kept one becomes `… 摘要.md` (what the interview reads — `transcriptDocumentIds` keeps raw transcripts out of its unread list), its facts and heard days become inferred profile facts citing `recording:<doc>`, and at most five of the person's own commitments become captures with ids prefixed `RECORDING_CAPTURE_PREFIX`, whose proposals `autoApprovalEligible` never lets take effect by themselves (no speaker separation: TV or another person's promise can read as the person's). Since 2026-10-20 two more things come of a kept recording, each gated on the person: at most `DIGEST_WAITS_PER_RECORDING` (3) promises someone else made to the person (`by: "other"`, with `who` as the transcript names them) become waiting items `wait-recording-<doc>-<n>` (`source: "recording"`, expected on the day named, else `DIGEST_WAIT_DEFAULT_DAYS` (7) after the recording and `approximate`) that only the person's answer closes; and a heard day **with a time** is read back from its `date_heard_*` fact by `heardEventFromFact` (`packages/core/src/fixed-events.ts`, one hour long, `source: "recording"`) and offered through the ordinary `event_propose` ask (`heardEvents` in `apps/web/lib/fixed-events.ts`), never written to a calendar by itself; the fact stays whatever the answer.
