# Opportunity agent: the time–space engine (2026-11-02, track ② of docs/opportunity-agent.md §17.9)

> Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-04. Add new notes for this area here, not to `CLAUDE.md`.

Whether an opening the person is eligible for can actually be attended: time, place, travel, visa and the calendar. Rules and the record of what was built: `docs/opportunity-agent.md` §17.6–17.9.

- **One pure function, two forms** (`packages/core/src/feasibility.ts`). `feasibilityOf(opportunity, FeasibilityContext)` (async lookups; the stable signature track ① calls) and `feasibilityNow(opportunity, SyncFeasibilityContext)` (lookups answered at once, for render-time reads such as `collectAsks`); `feasibilityOf` is `feasibilityNow` that waits for missed lookups and judges again, so both give the same verdict. Deadlines and starts are read in the zone their quote names (AoE, PST/EST, CET …; CST is +8 only in a Chinese quote); visa and travel lead times count back from the event when the deadline is earlier. No deadline and no start day (TBD, a year only) is not a deadline of today: feasible with `dateUnconfirmed` (additive), the card says 「日期待定」 and the next judgement after a date appears counts it (D10). Tests are counterfactual pairs (time-zone abbreviation, year end, leap year, too far, visa too late, TBD vs dated, fixed vs movable clash).
- **Every lookup is a ledger cache.** `geo.cache` (Nominatim answers and misses), `visa.cache` (verified readings `VISA_CACHE_DAYS`, unverified a week), `travel.trips` (mail trips), `location.device`. `packages/db/src/time-space.ts` (`cachedFeasibilityContext`, `feasibilityContextFor`) builds the context; its blocks are the calendar's plus `schedule.workBlocks`, which the web's attention timer writes (`recordScheduledToday`) because the worker cannot derive the schedule.
- **Geocoding** (`apps/worker/src/geo.ts`, `geocoderFor`): Nominatim, one request per 1.1 s for the whole process, an identifying User-Agent (`safeGet`'s new `userAgent`), only a city or a venue (`geoQueryAllowed` refuses street addresses, links, long numbers), reverse lookups city-level (zoom 10) from coordinates rounded to two decimals; an unanswered request is not cached. http, sleep and now are injectable.
- **Visa** (Skill `crossline-visa-finder`, low tier, web search; `packages/core/src/visa.ts`): the model gives only official page addresses and verbatim sentences (`validateVisaReading`); the worker opens each page (`safeGet`), `officialVisaHost` accepts only government hosts, `visaCheckFrom` needs the quote in the page text, and the days come from `processingDays` on the processing sentence (working days ×7/5, upper end of a range) plus `VISA_MARGIN_DAYS`. The model never states a lead. Unreadable → `VISA_LEAD_FALLBACK_DAYS`, `verified: false`.
- **Where the person is** (`packages/core/src/person-places.ts`): the device location (below), trips read from calendar titles (flights, 出差) and far single-day events, mail triage's new `travel` kind (`trip {city, country, from, to, quote}`, the quote must be in the mail, kept while the trip has not ended), and the confirmed home city fact (never 「假设：」).
- **The worker pass** (`timeSpacePass` in `apps/worker/src/time-space.ts`, `placePass` after the opportunity pass in `index.ts`, same owner gate and 规划 pause): at most 12 new geocodes and 2 visa readings a pass (visa needs core spend), then judges every open opportunity. **Only a clash with a fixed arrangement is said on its own** (`sayToPerson`, 08:00–23:00 local, ≤3 a day, each clash once, `opportunity.clashSaid`); a clash with work blocks only is shown on the card. **Each find is judged before it is kept** (`discoveryFeasibility`, passed by `opportunityPass` as `Clients.feasibility` to `runWatch` and the scout's `afterScout`): no preparation for an event, else the watch's `leadDays`; the watch's `radiusMinutes` is `localMinutesMax`; visa readings only with core spend; an `infeasible` verdict is held back (`holdBack`) and never reaches the person.
- **The offer card** (`apps/web/lib/opportunity-space.ts`, used by `opportunityOfferAsks`): judged with `feasibilityNow` from the caches; `infeasible` is not offered. It shows online/in person and the city, where the person is that day, distance, mode, one-way time and cost tier, the visa (verified or not) and one sentence per clash. A fixed clash adds 「我同意调整固定安排」, then asks how — move to another day, skip once, the person swaps or takes leave, or a sentence — kept in `schedule.fixedAdjustments` (undoable with the answer; no calendar is changed). 报名 / 加入计划 put the event's quoted hours into the plan (`scheduleEvent`), so the scheduler moves the work blocks it lands on.
