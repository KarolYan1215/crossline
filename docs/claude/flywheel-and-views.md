Design and decisions: `docs/flywheel-and-views.md`; its appendix keeps each phase's full implementation record. Built in parallel worktrees and merged.

- **Today's day panel (F1)** — `apps/web/app/timeline/quick-actions.tsx` `DayQuickActions`, `block-overview.tsx`, `block-start.ts`, pure `panel-model.ts`. The strip under the calendar (`aside.cal-detail`) is gone; the column beside the day view is where a block is looked at and acted on: 当日负荷 fixed on top, the picked block's task (or 下一项) below, links to 待批准 / 待决 / 可用时段 at the bottom. One start path: `TaskActions` takes `beforeStart` (`lockOnStart`). **The picked block's card has an input box** (2026-10-21): the day's one conversation (`Conversation variant="block"`) with `page` `{kind:"task", entityId:<order>, stepId:<the block's step>}`, showing only this task's last exchange with long replies folded; `PageContext.stepId`/`ConciergePack.page.stepId` are optional, checked by the turn route, and `pageTask` marks that step with ▶ (the first open step when it is done). 「下一项」 and calendar events show none. 完成并提交材料 opens the task's own completion form (`TaskCompletionSheet` in `app/task/task-completion.tsx`, the one the task page uses: one line, a link or files through `evidence-attachments` and `complete`, starting the timer first when needed); 推迟 is previewed as an impact `defer`. Week-view picks open that day. Details for the week's blocks come with Today (`calendarDetails`); others from `GET /api/work-orders/[id]/detail`.
- **自动交给 Agent (F1)** — `agent.autoHandoff`, default off (`isAutoHandoff`/`setAutoHandoff` in `packages/db/src/agent.ts`, `POST /api/agent/auto-handoff`). Governs calendar steps the planner judged system-doable (`executor: "mixed"`); the assistant's own `executor: "agent"` preparation steps stay automatic regardless. The schedule is derived, so the attention timer writes `schedule.stepStarts` (each step's earliest block this week); `autoHandoffTiming` calls a step due 30 minutes before its block. `agentService.pass` order: due auto step → preparation steps → whole orders → early auto step; quota and pause rules unchanged. A mixed step run clean under a trusted kind is now marked done (it never was).
- **Flywheel graph (F2)** — `apps/web/app/flywheel/` (`graph-model.ts` pure, `flywheel-graph.tsx`), data `apps/web/lib/flywheel-graph.ts`. Replaces Today's per-line cards. Anchors = each line's goal, dots = live milestones (radius by unfinished tasks, hollow when draft), edges member / chain / cross (approved solid, candidate dashed, decided in a popover through `/api/milestone-relations/decide`), R labels on loops of approved relations only. Hand-written force layout seeded from id hashes (same shape every load), drawn at the container's measured width. The e2e test scrolls the graph into view before using the wheel (it sits below the fold) and repeats the wheel until hydration.
- **Plan page (F3)** — no calendar any more (the plan is one calendar only on Today). `?horizon=4|8|far` (far = farthest approved deadline, ≤26 weeks). Laid out again on 2026-10-28 after `docs/plans/page-drafts-1028/plan.html` (mapping in `impl-c.md` there); one scenario: once a week or after the hours changed, are the coming weeks enough, does each line get its share, why does anything not fit. The page uses `AppShell` directly with its own head (title, horizon, the calendar sync/publish as icons, 「排程依据说明」 → `#advanced`) and the frame's `--ds-*` tokens inside `.plan-page`. Four figures (`apps/web/app/plan/plan-head.tsx` `PlanKpiRow`, from the pure `planKpis` in `lib/plan-stats.ts`): this week's usable and scheduled hours, what could not be placed (→ `#why`), and the estimate bias (median actual ÷ estimate, its scatter opened in a sheet; 「尚无实测」 in a word when nothing was timed; home in 系统体检). **No milestone axis** (scenarios.md conclusion 2): one line counts the horizon's milestones and those at risk / not ready / in the library (`milestoneSummary`) and links to 主线, whose markers now carry those states. Then three cards: capacity vs demand; 「各线排程 vs 份额」 (`lineShareRows`: each line's share of the coming weeks' usable hours beside the hours a week planned for it — the look back is the weekly review's, so the old planned-vs-timed histogram is gone); and 「排不下的原因」 (`UnplacedCard`, id `why`, from `unplacedItems` over `planRanking` and `unplacedTaskIds`): each task with its line, hours, deadline and the planner's reason, its one action 「加时段」 (→ the grid), push/drop linked to 本周承诺 (conclusion 3). Then hours and energy as one paintable week × half-hour grid over the full 24 hours (H/M/L in three depths of one teal, no tabs; 「复制到工作日」 copies the last painted day's row to Monday–Friday, `copyToWeekdays` in `week-grid.ts`) beside the last eight weeks' timed rhythm, with before/after when edited. 主线份额 (each line's goal as the name's hover) and 「高级」 (presets and the four packing numbers in boxes, the read-only ranking in a sheet behind 「排程顺序表」, the written explanation) are out of their fold. Every change still goes through `lib/impact.ts` before it applies; importance is set on each milestone (主线页) and due days on task cards. **Weekly energy advice** (`packages/core/src/energy-advice.ts` rules — rank-based bands on the three-process model at the person's phase, self-reports agreeing 2 of 3 in a slot win, timed rhythm only flags contradictions, no advice under 3 nights and 2 reports; `apps/worker/src/energy-advice.ts` + Skill `crossline-energy-advisor`, web-checked sources opened through `safeGet`, at most one level moved, never sleep hours): stored as a suggestion drawn over the grid with its reason and basis, 「批准建议并预览」 goes through `lib/impact.ts`. Previews return before/after chart data and the charts draw ghosts.
- **Identity attributes (F4)** — `packages/core/src/line-attributes.ts`. The interview returns `lineProfiles[].attributeCandidates: [{text, kind: domain|ability|state, sourceRef}]` (6–12, no containment duplicates, refs checked) instead of whole-sentence `goalCandidates` (still read); `goalEdits` carries 「把目标改成…」 as a card the person confirms (`POST /api/lines/goal-edit`). `apps/web/app/ui/attribute-picker.tsx` composes 「成为 A 的、B 的<身份>」 into `goalParts` + `goals[0]`. - **Artifact-first cross-line flywheel (F5/F6)** — `packages/core/src/artifact-plan.ts`, Skill `crossline-artifact-planner` (`crossline.artifact-plan.v1`, top tier), plan in setting `flywheel.artifacts`. **The separate relations run is gone** (`runRelations`, `MILESTONE_RELATIONS_SCHEMA`, `validateRelationsProposal`, `relationWarnings`): the artifact plan lists each line's artifacts and cross-line supply pairs (`transfer {metric, expected, unit}`, `lagDays`, `counterfactual`, `hoursSaved`); each architect run gets its line's pairs and plans gain `outputs[]`/`inputs[]`; relations are derived from references (`deriveArtifactRelations`, `syncArtifactRelations`) under four hard checks (artifact matched across different lines, time order, numeric transfer with unit, ≤2 outgoing edges per line ranked kept/approved → hours saved → transfer → id). Artifact ids start with their line id. Migration 13: `milestone_anchors.outputs/inputs`, `milestone_relations.artifact_id/transfer_json/check_on/counterfactual`. Redoing one line keeps other lines' artifacts and pairs approved milestones rest on (`keptArtifactPlan`) and no longer bulk-archives relations. Relations are approved with the downstream milestone (`saveMilestoneEdit`); drafts list their artifacts with `DELETE /api/milestone-relations/[id]`. Ask `relation_check` on the check day (兑现 / 部分 / 没有 → refuted), 系统体检 跨线关系兑现率, low-fulfilment kinds fed back as `lowFulfilment`. Timeline edges: upstream colour, dashed while candidate, red broken when time order no longer holds. `scripts/artifact-plan-replay.ts` replays one line on a ledger copy.
- **Model providers (R7, 2026-10-13; `docs/model-providers.md` §3).** Any model runs through **the same Codex path** (decided 2026-10-13: no direct adapters). A workspace's own provider is the `model.provider` setting (no key; `keyLast4` only) plus a DPAPI file `data/secrets/<ws>/provider-key.bin` (`packages/connectors/src/secret-file.ts`; refused off Windows, never in the ledger, exports or backups). Codex is pointed at the worker's loopback **model gateway** (`packages/codex/src/model-gateway.ts`): `base_url = http://127.0.0.1:<port>/<HMAC(workspace, provider) under a per-boot secret>`, the upstream looked up from the route, never from the request; the gateway adds the key per request, so no key is ever in a runtime's env, config, thread settings or rollouts, and a refreshed token needs no sidecar restart. `chat` providers (DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, GLM, Claude/Gemini OpenAI-compatible) are translated Responses↔Chat Completions by `chat-bridge.ts` (pure; namespaces flattened to `ns__name`, `<think>`/`reasoning_content` → reasoning items, `json_object` mode puts the schema in the system message); `responses` providers (OpenAI API, OpenRouter, Ollama) and `siwc-plan` are passed through. The provider is defined **per thread** (`thread/start` `modelProvider` + `config["model_providers.<id>"]` — proven to work by `scripts/provider-probe.ts`), with memories off: Codex's memory phases default to `gpt-5.6-terra` and retried it six times against Ollama. `requireLogin` is skipped on a provider route (a custom provider's `account/read` is `account:null, requiresOpenaiAuth:false`). `decideRoute` (`providers.ts`, called first in `executeSkill`): the provider when it has the capability (`capabilityGap` — third parties never get web search, images, the shell assistant, hosted connectors or memories) **and** its current conformance record admits the Skill; else the workspace's own Codex sign-in; else `MODEL_CAPABILITY_MISSING`/`MODEL_NOT_ADMITTED` in plain words, without starting a thread and never the owner's runtime. `SKILL_NEEDS` (`packages/core/src/provider-conformance.ts`) lists every Skill's label, schemas and needs — a discovery test fails for a Skill directory without an entry, and another for an exported `*_SCHEMA` missing from the conformance cases. Conformance runs test-only Skill `crossline-conformance` through the same gateway and repair loop over every schema plus long-text/`anyOf`/deep cases (critical gate and analysis ×3: 100% and ≤5% repairs; others ≥90% overall), writes `model.provider.conformance`, and goes stale with another model, another runtime binary hash, 30 days or a later save. **An OpenAI-compatible relay (中转站) is a first-class starting point** (2026-10-30; preset `relay` with empty address and model, 「用中转站接入」 on the card in Settings → 模型连接 and on `/connect`, tutorial tab 「中转站」 in `modelProviderGuide`, for someone with neither ChatGPT Plus nor an official API). Saving now **tries one real request first** (`apps/worker/src/provider-probe.ts`): the address is normalized (`normalizeProviderBaseUrl` strips a pasted `/chat/completions`/`/responses`/`/models`), every DNS answer must be public, then `GET /models` and one minimal request, no redirects followed; a failure is said in words (`PROVIDER_KEY_INVALID`, `PROVIDER_PATH_WRONG` with the `/v1` hint, `PROVIDER_MODEL_UNKNOWN` with the listed look-alikes, `PROVIDER_BALANCE`, …) with any key-shaped text masked, and nothing is saved; a passing save starts conformance at once. `providerConfigProblem` requires a public domain name (no IP literal, single label or `.local`/`.internal`-style suffix), and loopback is refused on the hosted beta (`allowLoopback`), both on save and in the gateway's `upstream()` (`refused` → 502 without connecting), which also re-checks DNS per request; the gateway never follows a redirect. A paid provider's quota reads `PROVIDER_BUDGET_UNKNOWN` (background pauses; the app knows no price), a loopback one is unmetered. `parseRoute` treats only a known effort after the last colon as an effort (`qwen3:8b` is a model). Real runs: `scripts/provider-probe.ts`, `scripts/provider-conformance.ts` (2026-10-13, local Ollama qwen3:8b: chat and responses each 33/34 with 0 repairs, 19 Skills admitted; only the 1500-character case timed out at the script's 20-minute turn limit; no cloud vendor measured yet). The SIWC token itself (OAuth, refresh) belongs to the login work and is not wired yet.
