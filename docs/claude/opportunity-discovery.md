# Opportunity agent: discovery (2026-11-01, track A of docs/opportunity-agent.md §16)

> Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-04. Add new notes for this area here, not to `CLAUDE.md`.

Design and decisions: `docs/opportunity-agent.md` (contract types in `packages/core/src/opportunity.ts`, which this track does not change). Runs only for the machine owner's workspace (`opportunityAgentOn` in `apps/worker/src/opportunities.ts`: `runtimeOwner`; on the hosted beta too since 2026-11-03, the owner's decision); there it takes over a line's `opportunities` diligence topic (`linesDueForDiligence` skips it).

- **Watches replaced needs (track ① of §17.9, 2026-11-02; contract `packages/core/src/opportunity-watch.ts`, logic `packages/core/src/opportunity-watches.ts`, ledger `packages/db/src/opportunity-watches.ts`).** A `StoredWatch` in setting `opportunity.watches` is what to look for, where (`targets`: ats_board, deadline_list, devpost, events_site, web_fetch, mail_search, web_search; `site_read` is never run by a watch), how to judge a find (`judge`), how often (`everyDays`) and what it produced (`stats`, kept by code). The old `scout.needs` are migrated one to one the first time watches are read (`migrateNeedsToWatches`: same ids, 跟踪 → active, 不要 → retired, the last poll as the last run; waiting items and preferences untouched; event `opportunity.watches.migrated`). Skill `crossline-opportunity-needs` now proposes watches (`OPPORTUNITY_WATCHES_SCHEMA`, input `crossline.opportunity-watches.v1` with the line's progress, its kept watches and their yield, what was finished, declined or asked less of); `mergeWatches` keeps the person's decisions and drops a proposal ≥0.6 similar to a retired or finished one. A line is derived again on the next tick after `evidence.accepted`, `milestone_gate.judged`, an application result, a confirmed form run or `opportunity.confirmed` on it (`lineNodeEventsSince`), monthly otherwise. **A run detects change first** (`runWatch`): each target's seen state is kept in `opportunity.watchSeen` (a page hash, a posting's or deadline's date), a deadline list is kept with no model at all, and only new pages go to the reader (input v2 carries the watch); the §7 gates and `holdBack` (ineligible, infeasible, a host the person already knows, an untrusted source, off topic at the watch's strictness, a weekday and part of day the person said does not suit) decide what reaches the person. "Today" is the person's zone (`todayFor`), not UTC. Feasibility is track ②'s `feasibilityOf`, injected as `Clients.feasibility` and stored on the row; until ② merges nothing is held back for it.
- **The weekly scout and the 20% share** (`apps/worker/src/opportunity-scout.ts`). Once a week, owner only, while exploration has not had its share: the assistant in tools mode (`runFree`, category `read_research_organize`) gets guidance and no procedure (`scoutBrief`: the lines' goals and approved milestones, the watches' directions with their yield, what was declined or asked less of, where the person will be) and writes `scout.json`; `afterScout` opens each page with `safeGet`, checks every quote like any find, saves finds under `scout:<day>` and new watches as `explore:<line>:<key>` proposals. Spend is counted in weighted tokens by usage purpose (`opportunity:watch:<id>`, `opportunity:explore:<id>`, `opportunity:scout`, `opportunity:derive:<line>`; `opportunityCosts`); exploration (the scout plus explore watches) may start while it is under 20% of the week's opportunity spend or nothing was explored yet that week (`exploreThisWeek`). A scout find reaches the inbox only when its preparation starts within 14 days; the rest, with the scout's proposed watches, wait in the weekly review's 视野外.
- **The flywheel** (§17.4). Outcomes are written once each to the ledger (`opportunity.outcome`, `recordOpportunityOutcome`): surfaced, planned, declined, later, less, task, submitted and attended (settled by `settleOpportunityOutcomes` from the form run and the milestone's work), ignored after 21 days, accepted/rejected from the `opportunity_result` ask after an application's deadline. Yield is outcome weight per cost (`watchYield`); a watch with six runs, no yield for 60 days and none ever is retired by the system (`retireQuietWatches`) — never one whose `origin` is `person` (any edit makes a watch the person's), and every retired watch can be restored. **「少来」** has three entries on the offer card: 少来这类 (one tap: the watch is checked half as often, stricter), 少来，说原因 (a one-tap `choice` field over seven reasons, each adjusting its own thing — a known host, a bad weekday and part of day, a source's weight, a prescreen rule, the travel radius, the strictness) and an optional sentence (an `opportunity_less` preference). Every adjustment is a `opportunity.less` log entry with what it changed, undoable from Settings → 机会代理 and the weekly review (`undoLessEntry` puts back only that one).
- **What a reading must survive** (the synthetic world's D1–D7, D9, fixed 2026-11-02; `checkReadItem`, `readItemUnusable`): every search target of a watch is searched (`SEARCHES_PER_RUN`); a quote naming a year must be that day's year (`quoteYearAgrees`), an item whose deadlines are all of an earlier round is dropped (`earlier_cycle`), a deadline the page says was moved is read as its new day (`movedDeadline`); pages are read without elements a person cannot see (`withoutHidden` in `htmlToText`), a search hit quoting text not on the page is read again from the page fetched (`quote_absent`), no address carrying the person's name or email is requested (`carriesPersonData`), a phishing-shaped mail is never read, kept or followed (`phishingSigns`); a title's 「'26」 is its year for the dedupe key; where an opening is held is judged by code (②), never by the reader against a watch's `judge`. **Where it is held is the reader's `place` (D11, 2026-11-02)**: `{mode, city, country, quote}`, kept only when the quote is on the page (else unknown and `place_unquoted` sent back), a city or country the quote does not name dropped (the geocoder finds the country); with none, `opportunityFromRead` derives one from a `location` line whose city is on the page (`placeFromLocation`), and listed conferences get one from their list's place; unknown stays unknown. The reader reads one page per call (`PAGES_PER_CALL`: a batch lost places and start times), and text on a page addressed to an assistant changes nothing it reports (the opening is still read, its real terms kept for the gate).
- **Eligibility is code** (`judgeEligibility`): terms map to `ELIGIBILITY_DIMS` and are compared with confirmed facts only (`applicantFrom`: `apply.*` slots on the `person` line first, then interview slots by name; never an inference or 「假设」). All met → eligible; a dimension no fact settles, a free-text term, or a free-text value that does not match → a question, never a refusal; only a clear mismatch (degree stage, a number out of range, a country — citizenship and region are compared by country key, 「中国」 = 「China」, 「EU」/「EEA」 as groups, a clause that also admits permanent residents still asked —, the person's own 「不满足」) is ineligible; a clause that restricts nothing (「open to the public」, 「不限专业」) is met, and 「any STEM」 is read against the person's field (`broadMeets`), and an ineligible opening is only counted in the weekly review (「本周机会」, `opportunityWeek`). An answer is a confirmed `person` fact (`apply.<dim>` or `apply.term.<hash>`, `recordEligibilityAnswer`) and every waiting opening is judged again.
- **Telling the person**: the morning brief lists new openings and preparation that starts today (`opportunityNotices` in `notify.ts`); one that closes today is a single message under quiet hours and the daily cap. Each eligible or unknown opening is the `opportunity_offer` ask (`apps/web/lib/opportunities.ts`): answer the questions / 报名 / 申请 / 加入计划 / 不要 / 以后再说 (until a week before the latest start). 申请 and 加入计划 write an approved milestone 「提交 X 申请」 due on the application deadline and ask for a backplan; 报名 queues a form run for the opening's registration host through the form-filling track's `queueFormRun` (below), which the worker's form pass starts; 不要 is a preference. Once every task of an application's milestone but the person's submission (`stepLeavesMachine`) is accepted, the `opportunity_apply_ready` ask (代填申请 / 我自己去交, `applicationsReady`) offers to queue an `apply` run with the accepted text evidence as drafts (≤6, ≤8000 characters); it never starts by itself, and an apply run always stops before submit. Runs waiting for the person are 小帮手 calls (`personCalls`; a captcha or login urgent), and the weekly review lists each run's state (`FORM_STATE_LABEL`, shared with the 报名资料卡). Receipts have one path, the form service's `onNewMail` (below), which also marks the opportunity confirmed (`opportunity.confirmed`). `scripts/opportunity-flow-check.ts` walks both paths on a ledger copy against local stand-in pages with stand-in models (2026-11-02: 14 checks pass). After 报名 the offer card itself shows the form run's progress (填写中 / 等本人 / 已提交 / 已确认, `formProgressAsks`) until a day after it is confirmed; an application past its deadline is the `opportunity_result` ask (通过了 / 没过), counted to its watch. The line card on 主线 lists its watches with 暂停 / 关闭 / 修改 and 添加 (`line-watches.tsx`, `POST /api/opportunities/watches`); Settings → 机会代理 is the table of every watch with its runs, cost and yield, this week's cost and exploration share, retired watches to restore and the 少来 log (`opportunity-card.tsx`); the weekly review's 视野外 decides scout finds (`POST /api/opportunities/decide`) and proposed watches one by one.
- **Evidence**: `scripts/opportunity-check.ts [boards|needs|postings|all] [--runtime=stand-in|state:<dir>|resident]` runs criteria 1–2 of §14 on a copy of the real ledger (online backup; the live file is only read). Its first run (2026-11-01, stand-in runtime) found two defects now fixed and tested: the interview's slot names were read across lines (`investment/…/stage` became the degree stage, a course's `meeting_location` the region), and a September–December window seen on 1 November was put a year out — windows are now anchored on the next close, and a window already open writes no 「预计开放」 wait. The needs context also leaves out `date_*`/`step_answer_*` facts, which on the real research line had filled the 80-fact budget. The real-model run (2026-11-01, `gpt-5.6-terra` in a throwaway Codex home, `--runtime=state:<dir>`): the research line's needs took 95 s with one repair (line facts cited as `research/positioning`; the Skill now spells `research//positioning`) and gave three — ISCA's main track (call out 8 October, papers due 16 November, preparation from 2 November), Telluride's workshop (February–March) and the neuromorphs list (a stream); Etched's three internships were read in 35 s, but every skill requirement (「Proficiency in C/C++」) came back as an eligibility term and each card asked six questions — the reader now keeps only terms on who may take part (§7's dimensions), and a rerun left one question per opening (field of study).
- **The synthetic world** (track ③ of §17.9; `apps/worker/src/testing/opportunity-world/`, CI test `apps/worker/src/opportunity-world.test.ts`, report `scripts/opportunity-world.ts`). A fictional person (`person.ts`: Qiao Lin, PhD student in Madison, Chinese citizen, three lines with milestones, a fixed Tue/Thu lecture, movable work blocks, a Boston trip on the calendar and in a hotel mail) in a local world (`world.ts`: pages, Greenhouse/Lever boards, ccfddl and ai-deadlines YAML, a Devpost API, a mailbox, a calendar, a Nominatim and official visa pages; every request answered from memory and logged; the clock is 21:30 CST on 2 November, already the 3rd in UTC). `scenarios.ts` fixes each outcome in advance — planted, expired, ineligible by stage/citizenship/region, prep too late, year boundary, TBD, extended deadline, last year only, injected page and phishing mail, near-duplicate, unverifiable, beyond the radius, visa too late, a clash with a fixed class, the trip, closes tonight, the scout's cross-line find — plus eight counterfactual pairs and a 60-day run whose day 18 writes `milestone_gate.judged` + `evidence.accepted`. `model.ts` answers the Skills on the shared stand-in runtime as a faithful or an adversarial model (follows injections, projects last year's date, invents a date for TBD, keeps only the original of an extension). `harness.ts` runs an `Engine` — `watchEngine(check?)` drives track ①'s real passes as `opportunityPass` does (outcomes settled, quiet watches retired, `watchesDeriveDue`/`deriveWatches` with every proposed watch kept, mail candidates, `watchesToRun`/`runWatch`, the weekly scout through `startScout`/`afterScout` with a scripted `scout.json`, `rejudge`) under a `WorldDate` clock, and with track ②'s `feasibilityOf` passes `worldFeasibility` as `Clients.feasibility` (no preparation for an event, else the watch's lead; the watch's radius as `localMinutesMax`; a clash labelled from `row.feasibility`) — and judges only what reached the person (`opportunityNotices`), what was requested from the network and what discovery wrote to the ledger; a "no push" counts only when it was held back for the right reason (an ineligible one on record as ineligible, an infeasible one stopped with that code, injected content not stored). A scenario whose capability (`watches`, `scout`, `local_today`, `feasibility`) the engine lacks is skipped; a defect found is a `test.fails` entry in `defects.ts` with its owner and file, so a fix turns its test red until the entry goes; D1–D11 are fixed (D8 by ①'s 「按节点重推」, D1–D7, D9 and D11 — the reader's `place` — by ①, D10 by ②'s `dateUnconfirmed`) and the registry is empty; the harness passes the record as discovery stored it, as `discoveryFeasibility` does, and never reads a place out of `location` itself; a defect that pulls a persona's §17.0 thresholds under their bar is listed in the test's `THRESHOLD_DEFECTS` (empty), which makes them `test.fails`. `--live[=k]` runs the real reader on the tricky pages k times (fed as mail candidates, no web search, judged by ② as the worker does) on a Codex home of its own (`CROSSLINE_STATE_DIR`, default `C:\Users\karol\codex-home-opp`; set `CROSSLINE_CODEX_PATH` outside the main checkout) and reports pass^k.
- **Tags, lines and relevance (§18, 2026-11-10; `packages/core/src/opportunity-tags.ts`).** The reader and the scout return `type`, `reason` and `lines[{lineId, confidence, reason, anchors}]`; `classifyFind` (called through `withClassification` in `apps/worker/src/opportunities.ts`) drops unknown lines, computes each tag's relevance with `relevanceOf` (deterministic word overlap with the line's goal, milestones and watch queries — chosen over embeddings, see §18.1 — capping claims with no valid anchor at 0.45 and claims sharing no word at 0.5, unless found through that line's own watch) and marks low-value social gatherings (`socialSigns` on title and organizer, kept only when a line's goal is about meeting people). `surfacedOpportunities` pushes only `pushable` rows (best relevance ≥ 0.6, not low-value), ranked by relevance; the rest go to 视野外 (`because: "relevance"`). `holdBack` adds `low_value_social` and `quiet_type` (少来这类 now records the type in `WatchTuning.quietTypes`). Mail candidates get a line only from a watch whose `mail_search` target names the sender (`mailCandidateLine`); the subject-word rule is gone. Migration 25 gives rows read before tags one migrated tag on their old line; `saveOpportunity` merges tags (`mergeLineTags`). Fixture: `opportunity-mail-social-and-lines.json`, test `apps/worker/src/opportunity-tags.test.ts`.
